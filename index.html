Draw a shape Pen tool

How the pen works
  • Click to place a corner point. Click and drag to place a smooth point and pull out its curve handles.
  • Click the first point to close the path.
  • Drag a point to move it. Select a point to see its handles, then drag a handle to reshape the curve.
  • Alt/Option drag a handle to break the pair for a sharp turn. Alt/Option click a point switches it between corner and smooth.
  • Shift keeps lines and handles at 45° steps. Delete removes the selected point, Ctrl/Cmd+Z the last one, and Enter adds the shape.
  • Fill closes the shape when you add it. Untick it to get a line instead.

Text or glyph shape
